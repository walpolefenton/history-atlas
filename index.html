    Reading the map

    Each colour family is a broad cultural region, and territories ruled by one power share a colour. Dashed outlines mark approximate borders. Large markers are key events; smaller dots are other recorded events and appear as you zoom in. The link in your address bar always holds the current year, selection and view, so you can share exactly what you see.
